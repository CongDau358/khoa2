What is Ultimate Frisbee?

Ultimate is a team sport played with a flying disc. Two teams try to advance the disc down a field and score by catching it in the opponent's end zone. It combines movement, strategy, throwing, catching, and communication.

The sport is known for the Spirit of the Game: players are expected to play fairly, communicate respectfully, and take responsibility for their own actions.

Basic rules

  • Players may not run while holding the disc; they establish a pivot foot and pass to a teammate.
  • A point is scored when a player catches a legal pass in the opponent's end zone.
  • Possession changes when a pass is incomplete, intercepted, or goes out of bounds.
  • Ultimate is generally self-officiated, so clear and respectful communication matters.
