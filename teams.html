How teams work

Ultimate is commonly played seven against seven on an outdoor field. Teams coordinate their movement to create passing options on offense and to limit open space on defense. Indoor and youth formats may use different team sizes.

Common responsibilities

  • Handlers: often initiate possession and make controlled passes to move the disc.
  • Cutters: make timed runs into open space to receive a pass and advance the disc.
  • Defenders: mark opponents, contest passing lanes, and help prevent completions.
  • Captains: help organize practice, communicate plans, and encourage fair play.

These are flexible roles rather than fixed positions. Strong teams adapt to the situation and support one another.

Finding a team

Beginners can look for school clubs, university recreation groups, community leagues, or local pickup games. Comfortable athletic clothing, water, and a suitable field are enough to get started; teams can usually provide a disc for a first session.

For information about organized play in the United States, visit USA Ultimate.
